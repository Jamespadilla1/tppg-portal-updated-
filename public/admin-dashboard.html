Promote to Team Leader

Are you sure you want to promote to Team Leader? Their past sales stay recorded under their agent-era history and won't count toward their new Team Leader commission — this is a clean break. They'll get a new TL-ID but keep their existing password.

This Team Leader will follow their Sales Manager's own reporting chain — no separate Unit Manager needed.

Promote to Sales Manager

Are you sure you want to promote to Sales Manager? Their past sales stay recorded under their Team Leader-era history and won't count toward their new Sales Manager commission — this is a clean break. They'll get a new SM-ID but keep their existing password.

Promote to Unit Manager

Are you sure you want to promote to Unit Manager? Their past sales stay recorded under their Sales Manager-era history and won't count toward their new Unit Manager commission — this is a clean break. They'll get a new UM-ID but keep their existing password. Unit Manager is the top of the hierarchy, so there's nothing else to assign.
